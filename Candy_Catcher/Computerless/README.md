# Queens of the Castle - Computerless Activity
This folder contains instructions and accompanying materials to run the computerless activity Queens of the Castle. This is typically run after lunch. 

*Duration* 
: 30mins 
<br>
*Core concept* 
: search algorithms, co-ordinates.
<br>
*Complexity* 
: Medium 	 
<br>
*Preparation* 
: Low 	 
<br>
*Space requirements* 
: if possible, a large open floor space where a giant chessboard can be created.  

#### Goal of game 
Students try to place n queens on a chessboard without any of them attacking each other. They work up to the 8 queens problem. 
Once they have found a solution in their workbooks, they will test it on a human sized chessboard! 


#### Preparation & materials
On the day, you will need to set up a giant chess board. Decide ahead of time how you want to do this (options are given in the lead setup doc) 
If you have many tutors, you may use them as queens on the giant board - alternatively, you can make crowns out of paper to place as giant pieces.  
Many of the resources have several options. Decide which materials best suit your space. 

#### Tips & notes
You may want to modify your version of the lead instructions - currently they include several options for how to set up the giant grid/run the game. Adjust your copy to just include what is relevant for your setup/equipment.
